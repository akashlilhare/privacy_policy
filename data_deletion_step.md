# Account Deletion – Moon (Winter Arc OS)

## How to Request Account Deletion

You can request permanent deletion of your Moon (Winter Arc OS) account and associated data directly from the mobile application.

### Steps

1. Open the **Moon (Winter Arc OS)** app.
2. Open the **Profile** section.
3. Scroll to **Security & Sovereignty**.
4. Tap **Account Sovereignty**.
5. Follow the on-screen instructions and confirm the deletion request.

Account deletion is permanent for the data covered by the deletion process.

## What Data Is Deleted

When account deletion is completed, the deletion process removes the account data supported by the application, including, where applicable:

- User account and profile information
- Authentication information associated with the account
- User preferences and settings
- App progress and activity data
- Cloud-synchronized user data
- Other personal data associated with the account

## Data That May Be Retained

Some information may be retained when required by law, security requirements, fraud prevention, accounting requirements, or other legitimate legal obligations.

Where data must be retained, it will be kept only for the applicable retention period and handled according to the application's Privacy Policy.

## Deletion Processing

The deletion request is started from **Profile → Security & Sovereignty → Account Sovereignty** inside the app.

The app asks for confirmation before deletion because the action is permanent.

For more information about data collection, use, storage, sharing, and retention, please see the application's Privacy Policy.
